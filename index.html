§06 — Questions

Things glass people ask us.

Can't find what you're looking for? Email kanch.help@gmail.com — we read every message.

Q.01Does Kanch handle mm and inches in the same invoice?+
Toggle units per invoice — chargeable rules, polish and rate calculations all follow the unit you're working in. Inches accept mixed fractions (e.g. 36 3/16); mm accepts decimals.
Q.02How do chargeable dimension rules work?+
Per glass type, define how the actual cut becomes the billed dimension: add a fixed value (e.g. +2″), round to a multiple of N, set a minimum, or combine them. Kanch applies the rule to every row and shows both actual and chargeable side by side.
Q.03Will my data sync across devices?+
Yes. One Kanch account works across desktop, tablet and mobile browsers. Invoices, customers, glass catalog and company details sync in real time, and pending edits flush on reconnect after a network drop.
Q.04Can I add my logo, GST and UPI details to PDFs?+
Your company profile holds your logo, address, GSTIN, bank details and UPI ID (with QR). Every PDF Kanch generates includes them, along with a calculations footer your customers can verify.
