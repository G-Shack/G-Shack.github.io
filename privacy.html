§01

Introduction.

At Kanch ("we", "us", or "our") we're committed to protecting your privacy and securing your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — in plain language.

your privacy matters We believe in transparency and giving you control over your data. This policy outlines our practices without legalese where we can avoid it.

By using Kanch you consent to the data practices described here. If you disagree with this policy, please don't use the App.

§02

What we collect.

2.1 — Information you provide directly

Data type Examples Purpose
Account Email address, password Authentication & account management
Company profile Company name, address, GSTIN, contact Invoice generation & business operations
Banking Account holder name, number, IFSC, UPI ID Payment details on invoices
Invoice data Product details, measurements, calculations Creating & managing invoices

2.2 — Information collected automatically

  • Device: device type, OS, app version.
  • Usage: features used, time spent in app, crash reports.
  • Technical: IP address, device identifiers, network info.
  • Performance: app performance metrics, error logs.

2.3 — Information from third parties

  • Firebase: authentication, database, and analytics services.
  • Cloud storage: file storage and synchronisation.
§03

How we use it.

3.1 — Core app functionality

  • Provide and maintain the invoice creation service.
  • Store your invoices and company data.
  • Synchronise data across your devices.
  • Generate PDF documents and reports.
  • Perform calculations and measurements.

3.2 — Account management

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Send important account notifications.

3.3 — Improvement & analytics

  • Analyse usage patterns to improve features.
  • Monitor app performance and fix bugs.
  • Develop new features based on user needs.
  • Generate anonymised usage statistics.

3.4 — Security & compliance

  • Protect against fraud and unauthorised access.
  • Ensure data security and integrity.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
§04

Sharing & disclosure.

important We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4.1 — Service providers

We share information with trusted third-party providers who assist us with:

  • Cloud hosting & data storage (Firebase, Google Cloud).
  • Analytics and performance monitoring.
  • Customer support services.

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.

4.2 — Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by law to:

  • Comply with legal process or government requests.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of Kanch, our users, or the public.
  • Investigate potential violations of our policies.

4.3 — Business transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We'll notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

§05

Security.

5.1 — Technical safeguards

  • Encryption: data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Secure authentication: Firebase Auth with industry-standard security.
  • Access controls: role-based access to data and systems.
  • Updates: continuous monitoring and patching.

5.2 — Organisational safeguards

  • Employee training on data protection.
  • Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regular security audits and assessments.
  • Incident response procedures.

5.3 — Your role

help us protect you Use strong, unique passwords. Keep your app updated. Don't share credentials. Report suspected security issues to kanch.help@gmail.com.
§06

Retention.

6.1 — Active accounts

  • Account data: retained while your account is active.
  • Invoice data: retained for business record-keeping purposes.
  • Usage data: typically retained for 24 months for analytics.

6.2 — Inactive accounts

  • We will notify you before deletion where possible.
  • You can request account deletion at any time.

6.3 — Legal requirements

Some data may be retained longer if required by law, for legal proceedings, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

§07

Your rights.

7.1 — Access & portability

  • View and download your account data.
  • Export your invoices and company information.
  • Request a copy of your personal data.

7.2 — Correction

  • Update your profile information in the app.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Modify company details and settings.

7.3 — Deletion

  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Request deletion of specific information.

7.4 — How to exercise these rights

contact us Use account management in the app, email kanch.help@gmail.com, or submit a request through our Support Center.
§08

Cookies & tracking.

8.1 — Usage

Our app and web services may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Provide authentication and security.
  • Analyse app performance and usage.
  • Improve user experience.

8.2 — Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with Kanch. Analytics data is anonymised and does not identify individual users.

8.3 — Managing cookies

You can manage cookie preferences through your device settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect app functionality.

§09

Children's privacy.

Kanch is designed for business use and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

if we discover this We will delete the information promptly, terminate the associated account, and will not use the information for any purpose.

If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at kanch.help@gmail.com.

§10

International transfers.

Your information may be stored and processed on servers outside your country of residence. We use Firebase and Google Cloud, which may store data in various global locations.

10.1 — Standards

  • Adequate data protection measures are in place.
  • Compliance with applicable data protection laws.
  • Contractual protections with service providers.

10.2 — Consent

By using Kanch you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your residence for the purposes described in this policy.

§11

Updates to this policy.

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or service improvements.

11.1 — Notification

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top.
  • Notify you through the app or via email for material changes.
  • Provide a summary of key changes.

11.2 — Continued use

Your continued use of Kanch after policy updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. If you disagree, you may discontinue using the app.
