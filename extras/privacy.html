1. Introduction

At Kanch Glass Invoice Maker ("Kanch", "we", "us", or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web services.

Your Privacy Matters: We believe in transparency and giving you control over your data. This policy outlines our practices in plain language.

By using Kanch, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

Data Type Examples Purpose
Account Information Email address, password Authentication and account management
Company Profile Company name, address, GST number, contact details Invoice generation and business operations
Banking Details Account holder name, account number, IFSC code Payment information on invoices
Invoice Data Product details, measurements, calculations Creating and managing invoices
Payment Information UPI transaction details Subscription management

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our app, we may automatically collect certain information:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Usage Data: Features used, time spent in app, crash reports
  • Technical Data: IP address, device identifiers, network information
  • Performance Data: App performance metrics, error logs

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party services we integrate with:

  • Firebase: Authentication, database, and analytics services
  • Cloud Storage: File storage and synchronization
  • Payment Processors: UPI transaction verification

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

3.1 Core App Functionality

  • Provide and maintain the invoice creation service
  • Process and store your invoices and company data
  • Synchronize data across your devices
  • Generate PDF documents and reports
  • Perform calculations and measurements

3.2 Account and Subscription Management

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Process subscription payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important account notifications
  • Verify payment transactions

3.3 App Improvement and Analytics

  • Analyze app usage patterns to improve features
  • Monitor app performance and fix bugs
  • Develop new features based on user needs
  • Generate anonymized usage statistics

3.4 Security and Compliance

  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Ensure data security and integrity
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Investigate and prevent misuse

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in:

  • Cloud hosting and data storage (Firebase, Google Cloud)
  • Payment processing (UPI payment verification)
  • Analytics and performance monitoring
  • Customer support services

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for specified purposes.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or in good faith belief that such disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with legal process or government requests
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of Kanch, our users, or the public
  • Investigate potential violations of our policies

4.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4.4 Consent

We may share information with your explicit consent for purposes not described in this policy.

5. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your information:

5.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Secure Authentication: Firebase Auth with industry-standard security
  • Access Controls: Role-based access to data and systems
  • Regular Security Updates: Continuous monitoring and patching

5.2 Organizational Safeguards

  • Employee training on data protection practices
  • Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security audits and assessments
  • Incident response procedures

5.3 User Responsibilities

Your Role in Security: Please help us protect your data by:

  • Using strong, unique passwords
  • Keeping your app updated
  • Not sharing account credentials
  • Reporting suspected security issues

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill legal obligations:

6.1 Active Accounts

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Invoice Data: Retained for business record-keeping purposes
  • Usage Data: Typically retained for 24 months for analytics

6.2 Inactive Accounts

  • Accounts inactive for 3+ years may be deleted
  • We will notify you before deletion when possible
  • You can request account deletion at any time

6.3 Legal Requirements

Some data may be retained longer if required by law, for legal proceedings, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

  • Improve user experience

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our app. This helps us improve functionality and user experience. Analytics data is anonymized and does not identify individual users.

You can manage cookie preferences through your device settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect app functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Kanch is designed for business use and is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13:

  • We will delete such information promptly
  • We will terminate the associated account
  • We will not use the information for any purpose

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at support@kanch.app.
